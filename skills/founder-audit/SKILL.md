# Founder Audit

A post-mortem on what's already live, so the next week's plan (founder-plan)
is based on what actually worked, not a guess.

## What to pull

For each post in the review window: post type (per founder-plan's
categories), hook formula used, engagement numbers the user can see
(reactions, comments, reposts), and impressions if available.

## Ranking

Rank by **engagement rate** (engagement divided by impressions) and
**reach multiple** (impressions divided by the user's follower count), not
raw impressions alone. A post that reaches fewer people but pulls a much
higher rate of comments and saves is doing more for distribution than a
high-impression post nobody engaged with, since comments and saves weigh
more heavily than impressions in what the algorithm pushes next.

## What to look for

- Which hook formulas and post types are actually earning engagement for
  this account, versus which ones the user assumes are working.
- Whether the first line is the drop-off point (low comments relative to
  impressions usually means the hook isn't earning the read).
- Whether posting time correlates with anything, though per founder-plan
  this matters far less than the hook.

## Output

A ranked table of the reviewed posts (best to worst by engagement rate),
one line per post on why it landed or didn't, and two or three concrete
takeaways to feed into the next founder-plan cycle. No numbers invented;
if the user hasn't supplied impressions or engagement counts, ask for them
rather than estimating.
