# Founder Carousel

Document posts (PDF carousels) get read slide by slide, which means each
slide has to earn the swipe to the next one. Most fail on slide 2, not
slide 1.

## Structure

1. **Cover slide**: the hook alone, big and short, same hook formulas as
   founder-post. No subtitle stealing its thunder.
2. **Slides 2 through N-1**: one idea per slide, numbered if it's a list,
   short enough to read in two seconds. Each slide should make sense with
   the ones around it removed, since people skip.
3. **Last slide**: the takeaway or CTA, stated plainly. If a link belongs
   anywhere, it goes here, or in a first comment, never in body copy.

Six to ten slides is the sweet spot. Beyond twelve, the swipe rate drops
off hard.

## Rules

Same voice rules as founder-post apply (no em dashes, no corporate
vocabulary, specific over impressive). Additionally:

- Every slide needs a reason to exist; cut any that just restate the last
  one.
- No slide should require the caption to make sense; the caption is a
  hook and context, not slide N+1.

## Output

Slide-by-slide copy, one slide per block, labelled by number, plus a short
caption for the post itself (separate from the slides, following
founder-post's hook rules). Note which slide is doing the least work, in
case the user wants to cut it.
