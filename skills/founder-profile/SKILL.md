# Founder Profile

A profile is the landing page for every comment, post, and DM. Score it,
then fix what actually costs points, in the order that matters most.

## Scoring (out of 100)

Score each, then sum:

| area | points | what earns them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | 15 | specific role + what the user actually does, not a title dump |
| Photo | 10 | real, recent, face visible, appropriate for the industry |
| Banner | 5 | not the default; says something about the work |
| About | 20 | opens with a real hook, not "I am a...", shows what happened not just who they are |
| Featured | 15 | 2-4 real proof points (post, article, product, press), not empty |
| Experience | 20 | specific outcomes per role, not a duty list copied from a job posting |
| Skills/endorsements | 5 | top 3 match what the user actually wants to be found for |
| Activity | 10 | recent posts and comments visible, not a dead account |

Give the score per section, then the total, then a one-line diagnosis of
what's costing the most points.

## Rewriting, in fix-first order

Fix in order of points lost, not the order sections appear on the page.
For each fix:

1. Show the current text (or describe it, if it's an image/layout issue).
2. Give the rewritten version.
3. One line on why the fix earns the points back.

Headline formula that works for most founders/operators: `[what you
actually do] at [venture] — [specific proof point or focus]`, not a string
of buzzwords or every title held. About section: open with a real moment
or number, not a bio; end with what the user is looking to connect on.

## Rules

- Never invent a metric, a title, or a company the user hasn't confirmed.
- No internal numbers in the About or Featured sections.
- Employee-with-ownership vs. actual founder changes what's fair to claim
  here too (see founder-post): don't rewrite a headline into founder
  language for someone who isn't one yet.

## Output

The score by section, the total, the fix-first list with before/after text
for each, and one flag if anything the user described can't be verified as
public.
