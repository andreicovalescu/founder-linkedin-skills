# Founder Inbox

A founder's inbox fills with recruiters, leads, peers, real asks, and spam,
in no particular order. Sort it before answering any of it.

## Categories

- **Lead**: a real prospect, investor, or partner reaching out about the
  user's actual venture.
- **Recruiter**: a job or hiring pitch, inbound or outbound.
- **Peer**: another founder/operator reaching out to connect, compare
  notes, or collaborate, no immediate ask.
- **Ask**: someone requesting time, advice, an intro, or a favor.
- **Spam**: templated pitch, irrelevant sales outreach, engagement-bait.

## Sorting signals

Flag the specific tell that placed each message in its category (a
templated opener, a generic "loved your profile," a specific reference to
something the user actually posted, a clear budget or role mentioned).
This is what lets the user trust the sort instead of re-reading everything.

## Output

Each message, one line: category, sender, the tell that placed it there,
and a suggested next action (reply now, reply later, ignore, or hand to
founder-dm for a drafted response). Group spam at the bottom without
drafting anything for it. Never send or archive anything; this is a sorted
list only.
