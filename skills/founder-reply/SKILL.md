# Founder Reply

The thread under a post is where a lot of the actual reach gets decided.
Reply to a high-value comment fast and it pulls more comments; ignore it and
the post dies at whatever it already has.

**This never auto-replies.** It hands back drafts, sorted by priority. The
user posts them.

## Sorting the thread

Pull every comment on the target post and sort into:

1. **Lead** — someone who could plausibly buy, hire, fund, or partner,
   signaled by their title, company, or what they said. Reply first, and
   make it worth a follow-up DM later, not a full pitch in public.
2. **Substance** — a real question or a real disagreement. Reply with an
   actual answer, not a deflection to "DM me."
3. **Peer** — someone at a similar level engaging in good faith. Reply
   briefly, add one real thing, keep the door open for reciprocity.
4. **Support** — encouragement, a one-liner, an emoji comment. A short
   genuine thank-you, not a template. Batch these; they don't need much.
5. **Noise** — spam, bait, unrelated self-promotion. Skip, or one
   dismissive-but-polite line at most.

## Drafting

- Lead and substance comments get a real reply, two to three sentences,
  answering what was actually asked or said.
- Never repeat the commenter's own words back to them as if agreeing counts
  as a reply.
- No fabricated experience here either, same rule as commenting on other
  people's posts: if the reply implies a result or a number, it has to be
  real and public.
- If a reply would reveal an internal or dashboard number, flag it and use
  `{{number}}` instead of inventing or pulling one from a connected tool.

## Output

The thread sorted into the five buckets, a drafted reply for each lead and
substance comment, and a short combined line for support comments. Note
which replies to post first for maximum visibility (earliest comments on a
still-rising post matter most). Never claim a reply has been posted.
