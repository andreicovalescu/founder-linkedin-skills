# Voice profile

Every skill in this pack reads this file before drafting anything. Fill it
in once, update it whenever something stops sounding like you. Ten minutes
now saves every draft after this from sounding generic.

Fastest way to fill it: paste 3-5 of your own real LinkedIn posts (or
comments, if that's closer to how you actually write) into Claude and say
"write my voice.md from these." Then read it over and fix anything that's
wrong.
